# 👋 Welcome to LaserJob

**LaserJob** is a parametric cutting plan generator for laser cutters.

## What is it?

A tool to create and manufacture custom objects in wood, plastic, or cardboard. You define the dimensions,
LaserJob generates the cutting plan, and your laser cutter does the rest.

## Use Cases

- **Board game store** → Custom boxes, game accessories, personalized storage
- **Jewelry shop** → Display cases, storage boxes, custom sets
- **Creators & Artisans** → Mockups, prototypes, unique pieces
- **Small businesses** → All possible use cases, directly adapted to your business

## 🚀 How does it work?

Choose a generator, adjust the dimensions and options, then your manufacturer can
produce it in less than 30 minutes.

Choose a category from the left menu to get started!

**Need custom development?** We can create specific generators tailored to your business.
